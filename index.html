CASE STUDY DETAILS

Challenge. Architecture. Impact.

01

Mobile proof-of-delivery & warehouse logistics

Challenge Paper delivery records could be misplaced, while scanning and uploading added administrative work and limited delivery visibility.

Architecture Custom Android application, QR-provisioned device access, secured REST endpoints, intermediate MySQL database, OCR-assisted delivery note matching, digital signatures and ERPNext Delivery Trip updates.

Impact Eliminated previously lost delivery notes (approximately 1 in 200), removed manual scanning/uploading and reduced administrative headcount by one.

02

Jamaican statutory payroll & tax engine

Challenge Standard payroll configuration required localization for Jamaican statutory calculations and reporting.

Architecture Custom DocTypes, server scripts and print formats for progressive PAYE bands, free-pay thresholds, annualization and rebate calculations; generators for TAJ SO1, SO2 and P24 returns; localized Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss formats.

Impact Automated localized payroll calculations and standardized statutory reporting outputs. Tax rules and thresholds should be validated against current TAJ guidance before each production payroll cycle.

03

Multi-location biometric attendance & overtime

Challenge Attendance from multiple sites needed consistent consolidation and overtime controls.

Architecture Bidirectional APIs connecting Hikvision terminals with ERPNext, automated attendance/lateness tracking and exception-based overtime approval routed to department heads before timesheet commitment.

Impact Centralized attendance visibility and introduced a controlled approval process; unapproved overtime reverts to scheduled shifts.

04

Shop-floor manufacturing & picking kiosks

Challenge Delays between physical production/warehouse activity and ERP records reduced operational visibility.

Architecture Real-time bidirectional kiosk interfaces for operators and pickers to record Job Card execution, machine utilization and pick-list completion, with validated stock entries triggered on task completion.

Impact Improved production and picking visibility while reducing reliance on delayed manual data entry.

05

High-availability ERPNext infrastructure

Challenge Business-critical ERP environments need resilience against individual host or service failures and a dependable recovery process.

Architecture Multi-host ERPNext deployment using Keepalived for virtual IP failover across frontend/database roles, with consolidated TrueNAS storage and hourly snapshots.

Impact Improved infrastructure resilience and established hourly recovery points. Actual database replication and failover behavior depends on the configured database technology and must be documented separately.
